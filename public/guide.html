Q1 · Understand it first, then choose how to play

What is the prediction game?

You are guessing AI Pipe’s total bill for 2–8 November 2026. Choose the money range you think will contain that seven-day total, and explain why.

It is the shared service’s bill, not your personal spending. Everyone predicts the same total. Your answers, timing, marks, and final result code are personal.

Example only: if you expect about $8 a day, seven days would cost roughly $56. You might choose $50 to under $100. The real bill is not known yet, so you can revise your prediction as new reports arrive.

UsefulYou can start now; do not wait for November 2.

Earlier participation matters for marks. You can leave all 1,000 practice credits unused. Buying or selling shares is not required.

1. How to play in the official game

  1. Open Q1 from your assignment. Follow your personal market link and check that you are signed in as yourself.
  2. Read the reports. Open See each day. Look at completed daily spending. Estimate the seven-day bill; during the target week, add the known days to your estimate for the remaining days.
  3. Select Your prediction. Choose the range containing your estimate. Keep Credits to trade at 0.
  4. Write Your reason. Say which facts support your choice and what new evidence would change your mind. Use at least 15 characters and three words, up to 1,000 characters.
  5. Click Save prediction. Check My answers for a row with your range and reason.
  6. Return when reports change. If new facts justify a different range, update your prediction and explain the change. Repeating the same answer is not a meaningful revision.
ImportantSave prediction is the button you need.

Spend credits, Buy shares, and Review trade are optional trading controls. Ignore them for a zero-credit prediction. An empty shares table is normal. “Latest facts: 0” rows are report updates, not your saved answers.

2. How to play with this solver

  1. Open My prediction. Data loads automatically. No token or file upload is needed for the numerical forecast.
  2. Check the report date and suggested range. Use Refresh prediction to request an update. With automatic checking enabled, the visible page checks every ten minutes.
  3. Read and copy the reason. Edit it to reflect your own thinking, then click Copy reason.
  4. Save in the game. Select that range under Your prediction, paste Your reason, leave credits at zero, and click Save prediction.
  5. Review later updates. The solver recalculates from new reports without an LLM. It does not change your official game answer until you save again.

Quick answers to common doubts

What are the 1,000 credits and shares for?

Practice trading. Spend credits and Buy shares are two ways to trade; Review trade previews one. You do not need them for the published Q1 marks. Saving a prediction alone does not create shares, so your shares can stay empty.

Why does the group show a different range?

The group figure comes from market prices; our forecast comes from spending reports. Neither is the answer key. A displayed 37% is not a count of students or a verified 37% chance of winning. Review the evidence instead of assuming the group or solver must be right.

Why do I see Latest facts: 0 and so many updates?

Before November 2, the target-week bill has not happened yet. Zero means no target-week total has been observed. Report updates are mixed into the game history. To confirm you saved, look for a row containing your selected range and your explanation.

Is this a random or perfect prediction? Does it need AI?

It is evidence-based, reproducible, and does not need AI—but it is not perfect. Four numerical methods use completed spending reports. The same data and calculation conditions give the same result; refreshing does not pick a random answer. Seeded simulations explore uncertainty rather than prove future accuracy.

There may be too few past weeks to show which method is best. That is why the solver may treat them equally. Once all seven completed target-day totals are present, it calculates their observed sum directly. The official game still issues your score and result code.

Does Refresh prediction also update my answer in the game?

No. Refresh checks available source data and recalculates the suggestion. An untouched solver draft follows new evidence, while your edits and AI-written drafts stay for review. Your official game answer changes only when you save again. No AI review is charged automatically.

Do I need a token or an imported file?

Neither is needed to get the numerical forecast. The AI Pipe token is only for optional AI review; the game token is only for account access and direct saving. Advanced data’s import accepts a previously downloaded forecast-evidence.json for analysis. You can ignore it during normal use.

How do marks work?

The published split is 10% participation, 45% correctness over time, 22.5% early entry, and 22.5% an explained change after new evidence. Starting late cannot recover the earlier scoring time. Trading profits do not improve marks. The official server determines the score.
